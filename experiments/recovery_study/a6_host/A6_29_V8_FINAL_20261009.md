## Video review

Each group has 14 videos: three scenes by four directions, plus the documented
vertical-board prone/supine procedural cases. Representative contact sheets at
0/4/8/12/16/20 seconds show:

- flat prone and supine roll or push into a kneeling/crouched posture but do
  not complete the transition to loaded upright standing;
- vertical-board cases remain pinned beneath the board with almost no useful
  clearance progress;
- free-board cases can rotate or displace the board and occasionally satisfy
  the geometric escape diagnostic, but the robot remains seated, kneeling, or
  folded rather than standing;
- G+ changes obstacle interaction details but does not solve the missing
  recovery-to-stand transition.

The result does not justify a longer continuation from either A6 checkpoint.
The next A6 source should only be selected after the V11/V13 posture branch
passes its independent standing and visual acceptance gate.
