# 29-DoF HoST A6 V15 evaluation report

Date: 2026-10-15

## Scope

V15 is a 29-DoF HoST A6 adapter acquisition experiment. It tests whether the
selected V37 standing actor can retain near-standing behavior while action
authority is reduced toward the formal A6 scale. It is not the complete paper
method and it does not satisfy the final A6 protocol.

The formal results below use the repository's 23-second diagnostic evaluator:
256 train-bank environments, 1,150 control steps, nominal dynamics, fixed
action scale `0.25`, primary standing held for three seconds, and geometric
escape reported separately. This evaluator is useful for within-adapter
comparison but is not the frozen 20-second SR1/SR10 cross-method evaluator.

## Conclusion and next step

V15 demonstrates a real acquisition result: it reduces action scale by about
`12.9%` while retaining continuous near-standing behavior in `98.44%` of the
deterministic audit cohort. It does not bridge to formal scale `0.25` and is
not a formal A6 success.

The training gate stopped even though the external uninterrupted audit exceeds
90%. The gate reads current uprightness during reset-time curriculum execution,
which can observe the newly reset pose rather than the pre-reset episode-final
pose. The next stage should cache the final retained predicate before reset,
verify that cache in a 64-environment preflight, and continue from update 800
with smaller `0.005` scale decrements and a longer hold interval. Middle, low,
and obstacle resets remain separate later stages; the G+ plate-center
separation reward must not be activated during flat near acquisition.

## V16 follow-up status

The reset-time gate fix now caches the final retained predicate on the last
controlled step and consumes that cache during curriculum reset. A
64-environment, 1,100-step preflight from V15 update 800 measured `98.44%`
retained cohort and `92.31%` mean retained-step fraction at scale `0.8123875`.
The scale remained unchanged during the required 14,400-control-step hold,
all target rewards and gradients were finite, and no NaN/Inf occurred.

Formal V16 training has started in
`logs/a6_29_v37_v16_cached_retention_4096_u2400_20261015` with `0.005` scale
decrements, three consecutive `90%` retained cohorts, and at least 600 updates
between decrements. This follow-up addresses the acquisition gate only and
does not change V15's `0/256` formal primary result at action scale `0.25`.
