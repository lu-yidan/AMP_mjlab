## Video review

Each group has 14 videos: three scenes by four directions, plus the documented
vertical-board prone/supine procedural cases. Contact sheets sampled at
0/4/8/12/16/20 seconds show:

- flat cases often reach a seated, kneeling, or hands-and-knees posture, but
  none completes loaded upright standing; several cases return to the floor;
- vertical-board cases remain pinned or folded beneath the board with no
  sustained recovery-to-stand transition;
- free-board cases can rotate, lift, or displace the board, consistent with
  the nonzero geometric diagnostic, but the robot remains seated, kneeling,
  prone, or supine;
- G+ improves board displacement relative to G-, but does not recover the
  missing transition from obstacle interaction to stable standing.

The V13 source therefore does not solve A6 under the frozen protocol, and the
two A6 checkpoints should not be continued into a longer training run.
