# 29-DoF HoST A6 V17 evaluation report

Date: 2026-10-16

## Scope

V17 is a 29-DoF HoST A6 adapter acquisition experiment. It accelerates the
cache-corrected flat near-standing action-scale homotopy validated by V16. It
is not the complete paper method and does not satisfy the formal A6 protocol.

## Gate plateau diagnosis

The scale plateau is a horizon mismatch rather than loss of long-horizon
retention. A deterministic preflight using the training episode length of 500
steps measured only `88.79%` retained cohort and `87.17%` mean retained-step
fraction across 4,096 environments, despite a `97.46%` episode-final
head-height pass. The same audit with 128 environments measured `89.06%` and
`88.12%`. Both are just below the unchanged `90%` gate and explain why the
scale correctly stopped.

After the 30-step motor-off interval, a 500-step episode has 470 controlled
steps. Requiring 80% retained steps permits only about 94 non-retained steps,
so slower but successful recoveries fail the gate. The current uninterrupted
audit instead covers 1,120 controlled steps and shows `98.44%` cohort
retention. The policy therefore retains standing once recovered, but the
short training episode conflates recovery latency with long-horizon
retention.

## Conclusion and V18

V17 is a clear migration improvement over V16: it reduces action scale by
`0.105` while preserving `126/128` uninterrupted retention. It remains far
from formal scale `0.25`, so no formal G-/G+ success claim, 2,048-environment
evaluation, or obstacle video is warranted.

V18 changes the measurement horizon instead of weakening the thresholds. It
uses 1,150-step training episodes, matching the uninterrupted near audit,
while retaining the `0.005` decrement, three consecutive `90%` cohorts,
80%-of-steps rule, 2,400-step hold, and all V17 optimization settings. A
64-environment, 2,400-step preflight from the V17 final checkpoint passed with
`98.44%` retained cohort, `93.82%` mean retained-step fraction, unchanged
start/end scale `0.6923876`, finite rewards and gradients, and no NaN/Inf.

Formal V18 training uses 4,096 environments for 14,000 updates in
`logs/a6_29_v37_v18_horizon_aligned_4096_u14000_20261016`. Middle, low, and
obstacle stages remain disabled until flat near-standing retention is stable
at or near action scale `0.25`.
