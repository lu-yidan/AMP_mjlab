# 29-DoF HoST A6 V16 evaluation report

Date: 2026-10-15

## Scope

V16 is a 29-DoF HoST A6 adapter acquisition experiment. It verifies the
pre-reset continuous-retention cache and continues the flat near-standing
action-scale homotopy. It is not the complete paper method and it does not
satisfy the formal A6 protocol.

- Optimizer state: actor, critic, optimizer, and normalizers resumed
- PPO: std `0.04`, learning rate `7.5e-6`, two epochs
- Resets: flat natural near-standing only, nominal dynamics, no obstacle reward
- Scale rule: decrement `0.005` after three consecutive retained cohorts at or
  above `90%`, with at least 14,400 control steps (about 600 updates) between
  decrements
- Retention rule: height `>0.70 m`, uprightness `>0.80`, at least `80%` of
  controlled steps, and retained at episode end

The gate caches the episode-final retained predicate during the final
controlled step and consumes the cache at reset. It therefore no longer reads
the new reset pose when deciding whether the previous episode passed.

Training completed normally without OOM, NaN/Inf, velocity termination, or
abnormal exit. Final mean reward was `85.77`, value-function loss was `0.0295`,
and surrogate loss was `0.0038`.

## Conclusion and next stage

V16 confirms that the cached reset-time gate is correct: it authorizes three
controlled scale reductions while deterministic uninterrupted retention stays
at `98.44%`. This is clear scale-migration progress, but scale `0.7974` remains
far from the formal `0.25`; no formal 256/2,048-environment success claim or
obstacle video is warranted.

The 600-update hold is now the dominant rate limit rather than policy failure.
V17 therefore keeps the `0.005` decrement, three-cohort `90%` retention gate,
and all V16 optimization settings, but shortens the minimum hold to 2,400
control steps (about 100 updates). The retained-cohort gate remains the safety
stop. V17 is still flat near acquisition; middle, low, and obstacle stages
remain separate and may start only after stable near retention approaches
formal scale `0.25`.

A 64-environment, 1,100-step V17 preflight from the V16 final checkpoint
passed with `98.44%` retained cohort, `92.07%` mean retained-step fraction,
finite rewards and gradients, and unchanged start/end scale `0.7973875`.
Formal V17 training uses 4,096 environments for 6,000 updates in
`logs/a6_29_v37_v17_accelerated_cached_4096_u6000_20261015`.
